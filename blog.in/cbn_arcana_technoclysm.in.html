I have ported my Arcana Technoclysm mod from Cataclysm: DDA to Cataclysm: Bright Nights. The port is now available as Arcana Technoclysm for Cataclysm: Bright Nights.

Arcana technoclysm is a mod building on top of the base Arcana mod. Arcana technoclysm adds some new magical-technical locations and items to the game, including summonable vehicles and electrical power sources.

The parent Arcana mod is no longer developed for DDA, so I ported my mod to Bright Nights where Arcana is included in the repository.
